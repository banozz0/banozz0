## 🚀 Shipped

- 📨 **[telegram-tools](https://github.com/banozz0/telegram-tools)** ![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/telegram-tools?style=flat-square&logo=pypi&logoColor=white&label=) — `pip install telegram-tools` · a local CLI for **your own Telegram**: the real IDs of your groups, channels and forum topics, message search/export, clearing a forum topic without deleting it, and editing your bots without `@BotFather`. Nothing leaves your machine.
- 🚦 **[waze-alert-telegram-bot](https://github.com/banozz0/waze-alert-telegram-bot)** ![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/banozz0/waze-alert-telegram-bot?style=flat-square&label=) — text your own bot **`/police`** and get the police reports, jams, accidents and closures in your area *right now*, plus `/route` and `/eta` checks that price in the traffic. Self-hostable, zero dependencies. Not affiliated with Waze.
- 🖤 **[minimal-terminal-card](https://github.com/banozz0/minimal-terminal-card)** ![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/banozz0/minimal-terminal-card?style=flat-square&label=) — an **agent skill** that turns notes, ideas and workflows into jet-black terminal-style PNG cards : diagrams, tables and process cards — the text-heavy images that image generation usually mangles.
- 🎯 **[acca-tracker](https://github.com/banozz0/acca-tracker)** ![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/banozz0/acca-tracker?style=flat-square&label=) — an **Agent skill**: send it a photo of your bet slip, confirm the legs, and it tracks each one from public score data — alive, dead, won, pending. Read-only, no betting advice.
